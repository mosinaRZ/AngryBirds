## 3. Game design (the one level)

- **Scene:** side view. Flat ground, a slingshot on the left, a structure of blocks on the right with enemies inside/on top of it.
- **Birds:** 3 birds per attempt, launched one after another. Each bird is a circle body.
- **Enemies:** 2–3 enemies. Each is a circle body with HP. Its **head** is drawn with `enemy_head.png` (circular, swappable file).
- **Blocks:** rectangles of three materials: wood, stone, glass (different density and HP).
- **Launch:** drag the bird back from the slingshot anchor (clamped to a max radius); release to fire with velocity proportional to the pull vector. Show a rubber band while dragging. A dotted trajectory preview while dragging is optional (Part 6).
- **Damage:** based on collision impulse (from `ContactListener.postSolve`). Blocks and enemies lose HP; at 0 HP they are removed (after the physics step).
- **Win:** all enemies destroyed. **Lose:** no birds left and the world has been settled (nothing moving above a speed threshold) for ~2 seconds while enemies remain.
- **Score:** points for each destroyed enemy/block, bonus for leftover birds.
- **UI:** HUD (score, birds left), result overlay with Retry. A simple main menu with Play.

## 5. Technical conventions

### 5.1 Units and camera
- World size: **23.4 m × 10.8 m** minimum, `ExtendViewport(23.4f, 10.8f)` (Part 12: the level of the reference screenshot is 23.4 m wide; changed from `FitViewport` in Part 10 so the game fills the whole screen with no side bars; the camera is centered horizontally on the 16 m world and anchored to the ground).
- 1 world unit = 1 meter. Sprites are drawn in world units (size set in meters). No pixel-based physics.
- Ground top is at `y = 1f`. Slingshot anchor is around `x = 3f, y = 2.5f` (finalized in Part 2 and recorded).
- Camera: fixed during aim; Part 5 may add a follow camera that clamps to level bounds.

### 5.2 Fixed timestep
```
accumulator += min(delta, 0.25f)
while (accumulator >= STEP) { world.step(STEP, 8, 3); accumulator -= STEP }
```
`STEP = 1/60f`. After the loop, process the deferred queue (removals, damage application, state checks).

### 5.3 Damage model
- In `postSolve`, read `ContactImpulse.normalImpulses`, take the max, and enqueue `(entityA, entityB, impulse)`.
- After stepping, apply: `damage = max(0, impulse - threshold(material)) * factor(material)`.
- Thresholds/factors live in one config object (`Materials.kt` or inside `Block.kt`) so they can be tuned in one place.
- Entities with `hp <= 0` are queued for removal; bodies destroyed only after the step.

### 5.4 userData
- Every body's `userData` is its `Entity`. Ground uses a dedicated `GroundEntity` (or null-safe handling).

### 5.5 Input
- Use `InputAdapter` + `camera.unproject` to convert touch to world coordinates.
- Only allow dragging when the current bird is on the slingshot and state is `PLAYING`.
- Clamp drag distance to `MAX_PULL` (about 1.8 m).
- Launch impulse = `pullVector * -POWER`, `POWER` tunable constant.
